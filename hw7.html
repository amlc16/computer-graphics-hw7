HW 7 Ray Tracing: F1 Night Pit Lane

Everything on screen is ray traced in the fragment shader: an F1 car built from boxes, ellipsoids and cylinders, a glass sphere, a reflective green cube, a polished checkered floor and a pit-garage backdrop.
Drag the canvas to orbit the camera. Edit the shaders on the left and the canvas updates as you type.
Sliders: bounce depth, ambient fill, light intensity, surface shininess and glass refractive index.
